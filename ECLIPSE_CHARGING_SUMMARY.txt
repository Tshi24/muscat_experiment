  "Near Bennu, charging is minimal in sunlight but in eclipse (no sunlight)
   spacecraft charges to ~-15V. Should we switch ON thruster to mitigate?"

ANSWER: ✅ YES - Enable eclipse protection

================================================================================
                         QUICK ANSWER
================================================================================

SET: policy_require_sunlight_for_ep = false

BEHAVIOR:
  • At current -15V eclipse: Thruster stays OFF (above -20V threshold)
    → No power consumption
    → Acceptable risk level
  
  • If eclipse worsens to ≤-20V: Thruster turns ON
    → Critical protection activated
    → Worth the battery drain for safety

  • In sunlit conditions: No change
    → Same threshold-based logic applies

RESULT: Smart, comprehensive protection with minimal power impact
